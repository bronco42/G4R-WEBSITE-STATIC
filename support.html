Contact

For help with the Garage Reorder (G4R) iOS app, email hello@ai4dat.com. We aim to respond within two business days.

Please include your iOS version, the app version (Settings > About inside the app), and a short description of the problem.

Common questions

Where is my data stored? All vehicles, items, photos, and reminders stay on your device. There is no server account, so deleting the app deletes your data.

How do I sign out? Open Settings inside the app and choose Sign Out. Your vehicle data remains on the device.

How do reorder links work? Each item carries an Amazon search link matched to your vehicle. As an Amazon Associate, 4DAT LLC earns from qualifying purchases. Always confirm part fitment for your specific vehicle before ordering.

How do I manage my Premium subscription? Subscriptions are billed and managed by Apple. Open iOS Settings > your name > Subscriptions to change or cancel.

Reporting an affiliate link issue

Include the vehicle, item category, the destination URL, and whether the tag=4dat-20 parameter appeared once.
