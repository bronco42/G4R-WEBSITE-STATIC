Why G4R exists

Garage order tracking that makes reordering a snap. G4R tracks each vehicle's replacement items (wipers, filters, key fob batteries, and your own categories) across your whole fleet, from cars and trucks to boats, jet skis, and e-bikes, reminds you when they're due, and keeps a record of every part you order and install.

The 4DAT way

Every 4DAT app is built on the same few rules. Here is how G4R follows them:

  1. Your information stays on your device. Your vehicles, items, schedules, notes, order history, and photos are stored on your device, and on iPhone they also sync to your own private iCloud. Nothing reaches our server unless you create the optional G4R Account for Android-and-iPhone sync.
  2. Accounts only where needed. On iPhone, G4R asks you to sign in with Apple or Google once, to create an account record kept on your device. On Android, no sign-in is needed. The G4R Account for cloud sync is optional.
  3. We keep only what a feature needs. Only if you create a G4R Account, our server (run by 4DAT on Cloudflare) stores your sign-in ID, email, device list, and your garage data and photos to keep your devices in sync. It is not end-to-end encrypted, and you can delete it all at any time at garage4reorder.com/account. See the Privacy Policy.
  4. No ads and no tracking of you. No ads, no analytics, and no tracking SDKs in the app, and this website loads no analytics scripts.
  5. Supported honestly. The app is free. Premium is an optional subscription that raises the vehicle and item limits and unlocks cloud sync. Reorder links open Amazon. As an Amazon Associate, 4DAT earns from qualifying purchases.
