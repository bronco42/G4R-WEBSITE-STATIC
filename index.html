What the app does

A running record of garage orders

Every order you track — from link opened to ordered, delivered, and installed — is saved to that vehicle's order history. The record lives on your device and builds up over time, so you always know what part you bought, when you installed it, and what to reorder next time.

Reordering in a snap

Each item carries an Amazon search link matched to your vehicle's year, make, and model. When something comes due, it's one tap from reminder to reorder.

Rate parts in your notes

Use an item's notes to rate the product on cost and quality — your notes are saved into the vehicle's order history when the part is installed, so your next reorder decision is already made. Structured cost and quality ratings are planned as a future app feature.

Scheduled, color-coded, private

Overdue, due-soon, and on-order status at a glance, local reminders when replacements come due, and all data stored on your device — no server account.

Your whole fleet, one garage

Add cars, trucks, motorcycles, boats and jet skis, even e-bikes. Track as much as you want per vehicle: VIN or serial number, license plate, insurance policy and expiration, and warranty coverage — all optional, all on your device.
